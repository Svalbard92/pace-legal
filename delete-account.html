Delete your Pace account

Pace for Android, by Wiseloop

Three ways

1. In the app

Settings › Account › Delete account, then your password. It is done at once.

2. On the web, without the app

Delete my account — your username and password. Your password stays in your browser; only a key made from it is sent.

3. By email

If you no longer remember your password, write from any address to kpchetia@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Pace account" and your username. We reply to confirm it is yours, then delete it within 7 days.

What is erased

  • From Pace's server, at once: your username, your password's hash, your plan, your sign-in sessions and your phones' push tokens. Your other phones are signed out. A licence key you used forgets it was yours, and our log keeps no name.
  • Copies of the account database we keep to undo mistakes are replaced as new ones are made (we keep the 60 most recent); your account leaves them that way.

What is not touched

  • What is on your phone. Pace never sent it to us. To erase it, uninstall Pace or clear its data in Android's settings.
  • Backup files you made. They are yours, wherever you keep them; delete them there. A backup restores only under an account with the same username, with the password it was made with.
  • A paid plan bought through Google Play: cancel its renewal in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions; deleting the account does not cancel it.
